# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Travellers collecting inspiration from social posts, articles, and recommendations who want to turn scattered saved places into an organized, practical trip.

## Product Purpose

Argo turns saved travel content into mapped locations, collections, and day-by-day itineraries. Success means users can move from inspiration to a feasible trip without manually reconciling opening hours, travel time, meals, and location order.

## Brand Personality

Approachable, playful, and dependable. The interface should make travel planning feel lighter while remaining calm and trustworthy during detailed planning work.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic corporate travel dashboards, dense booking-engine interfaces, ornamental dark-mode technology aesthetics, and visual changes that conflict with Argo's sticker-led identity or semantic design tokens.

## Design Principles

- Turn inspiration into an obvious next action.
- Keep complex planning decisions understandable and reversible.
- Use personality to make travel feel inviting without obscuring utility.
- Reuse familiar interaction patterns across links, collections, itineraries, and flights.
- Surface progress, limitations, and failures in plain language.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Support keyboard navigation, visible focus states, readable contrast, semantic labels, reduced motion, and responsive layouts across phone and desktop surfaces.
