Two Next.js client pages under `src/app/links/` form the feature: `page.tsx` renders the links list (bento grid of queue cards morphing into completed LinkCards via optimistic handoff keyed by `content_id`) and `loading.tsx` provides skeleton UI; `[id]/page.tsx` renders the detail view that fetches content via `getContentDetail`, maps rows to `LocationItem`s, and exposes rubber-band selection, batch save-to-collection/itinerary, and an async itinerary-generation flow driven by a second `useJobsQueue` subscription (`type: 'itinerary-planning'`). A thin `layout.tsx` wraps both pages in `MainLayout`. Data fetching is split between React Query hooks (`usePaginatedContent`, `useLinkUsageQuery`, `useInfiniteScroll`) for the list and direct Supabase calls from the detail page. All user-facing errors are surfaced through `useToast` and `useQuotaGate`, and job lifecycle events (`onJobCompleted`, `onJobFailed`, `onJobRejected`) drive optimistic state transitions and navigation.
