# Argo Owl Character Lock

Use `argo-owl-character-reference-sheet.png` as the primary visual reference for future generations. The individual files in `pose-crops/` are convenience references for specific angles and actions.

## Canonical appearance

- Squat, rounded coral owl body: `#EF6351`
- Large connected heart-like face mask: `#F7EEE2`
- Rounded three-lobed wings: `#F7EEE2`
- Large vertical oval eyes and small teardrop beak: `#20272B`
- One small white catchlight in each eye
- Soft, slightly asymmetrical ear tufts
- Short coral legs with simple three-toed mustard feet: `#E7B94E`
- Friendly, naive, slightly irregular handmade silhouette
- Flat vector-like shapes with no outlines, texture, or realistic feather detail

## Proportion rules

- The body is wider than the face mask and only slightly taller than it is wide.
- The face mask occupies roughly the upper half of the body.
- The eyes stay widely spaced and vertically oriented.
- The beak remains centered, small, and lower than the eye centers.
- Wings read as soft feathered paddles, never human hands.
- Legs remain very short; feet should not dominate the silhouette.
